CRAFTSTEP — HANDMADE SHOES WEBSITE

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Responsive homepage and product collection
- Men / Women / Sandals product filters
- Add-to-bag cart, quantity counts, remove items and subtotal
- WhatsApp order handoff
- Our Story, Craft, newsletter layout and contact footer

QUICK START
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview.
3. Edit script.js:
   - Replace WHATSAPP_NUMBER with your WhatsApp number (country code, digits only).
   - Change product names, prices, categories and image URLs in the products array.
4. Edit index.html:
   - Replace CraftStep with your real brand name.
   - Update email and Instagram links.
   - Update shipping promise and all marketing claims to match your actual business.
5. Replace the sample Unsplash photos with your own shoe photography before selling.

PUBLISHING
Upload index.html, styles.css and script.js to your hosting provider's public web folder (often named public_html), or deploy the folder using a static website host.

IMPORTANT
This is a polished front-end starter store, not a complete hosted commerce backend.
- WhatsApp checkout sends an order enquiry; it does not take card payments.
- Product inventory, customer accounts, order database, shipping integrations, tax and payment gateway are not connected.
- The newsletter form is a visual demo until connected to an email marketing service.
- Add your real business contact details, return policy, privacy policy, delivery policy and accurate product descriptions before launch.
- Product photos are sample remote images. Use photos you own or have permission to use.
